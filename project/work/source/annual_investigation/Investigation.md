# BitLife Community Systems v4 — where to find the additions

Install **BitLife_Community_Systems_v4.apk** over your working v3 build. The full
APK is about **257 MiB / 269 MB**, not a small patch file. If your download comes
out around 200 KB, use the ZIP backup, extract the APK and install that file.
Keep your current app installed so its saves stay available to the update.

Open a character and wait a moment. Tap the blue **MOD+** button near the upper
right of the game. This is the entrance to all the new activity screens. The
original SoundCloud and Job lists still use their original activities; the
expanded systems are in MOD+.

This build uses the character's actual identity, age and occupation to choose
and save progression. **Expansion credits, followers, releases, portfolio ranks,
certificates and racing/TV progress are separate from the main game's values.**
The bridge reads the game; it does not change its bank balance or native job
promotions. A new expansion life starts with 25,000 credits and 30 activity
points. Ageing restores 30 points and settles expansion accounts.

## Music studio and independent artists

**MOD+ → Music studio & independent label**

- Create and name a single, EP or album; choose its sound.
- Complete writing, production and mixing sessions. Choose a focused session,
  hire help, or experiment with a less predictable outcome.
- Collaborate during development, practice, improve production skills and
  upgrade the studio through five equipment levels.
- Choose distribution: independent releases keep 100% of their masters;
  distribution partners keep a 75% share with more reach; record labels offer
  an advance and wider reach in return for a 35% share.
- Choose organic promotion, a playlist campaign or a launch campaign.
- Track each release's quality, genre, ownership, lifetime streams and royalties.
  Old releases continue earning, with reach declining over time.
- License beats, pitch playlists and play shows after releasing music.
- Build a reputation and catalogue to found a label, then scout new artists.

These are selectable studio actions with saved project stages. An unfinished
project remains when you close and reopen the game. This expansion catalogue
is independent of the original SoundCloud Track List.

## Social media and creator business

**MOD+ → Social media & creator business**

SoundCloud, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Podcast and Fan Club each have an
expansion audience and trust rating. Choose behind-the-scenes posts,
collaborations, disclosed sponsorships, live Q&As, paid series or controversy
moderation. Sponsorships need at least 500 followers; paid series need 250.
Audience trust affects annual audience retention and income. Paid subscribers
bring recurring annual revenue. Music releases can grow the SoundCloud audience
and TV exposure can grow the YouTube audience.

## Work projects and portfolios

**MOD+ → Work projects & career portfolio**

An active regular main-game job is required. Assignments adapt to its name:
creative/media work, technical work, public service, education or business.
For example, a Reality TV Producer can develop a pilot episode, an artist
session or a festival broadcast. An engineer gets prototype, data or safety
projects. Complete planning, execution and review, then submit the assignment.
Quality and burnout affect the resulting project score and expansion bonus.

Specialist workshops improve skill; mentoring improves reputation; boundaries
reduce burnout. Complete projects and earn reputation to advance your saved
portfolio rank. The original Job menu and main-game promotions remain available.

## School, clubs and Sports+

**MOD+ → School, clubs & sports academy**

School-age characters receive enrichment activities; adults use community or
adult education. Study Creative Arts, Music Production, Coding, Media Studies,
Engineering, Business or Sports Science. Knowledge and attendance affect an
assessment score; a passing assessment earns an expansion certificate and
bursary. Build Debate, Robotics, School Radio, Drama, Songwriting or Student
Newspaper club projects and take developed projects to competitions. Support
peers or discuss your timetable with a tutor.

Sports academy activities include Football, Basketball, Tennis, Athletics and
Swimming training, regional tournaments and youth coaching. Sporting skill,
competition results and trophies persist. These enrichment scores and
certificates belong to MOD+, rather than replacing the school's original grades.

## Motorsport+ — where F1 is

**MOD+ → Motorsport • karting to Formula 1**

Start in **Karting** from age six. Progress through **Regional Formula → Formula
3 → Formula 2 → Formula 1** by earning skill, reputation, experience and licence
fees. Formula contracts begin at age 14; Formula 1 requires age 18 or older.
You start at the bottom even if your main-game character already has a job
with a racing-related title.

Each championship contains **12 rounds and 20 drivers**, including your
character. Every race has a qualifying session, forecast, grid position,
strategy and tyre choice. Attack can improve pace but increases mechanical
risk; conservative driving trades speed for a safer approach. Wet tyres matter
in rain. Car development improves pace, safety sessions repair reliability,
and engineers support performance. Mechanical retirements score zero.

Choose between five fictional teams with different entry requirements,
negotiate two-year seats, seek sponsorship and build paddock relationships.
Contracts pay annually and expire. Championship points, opponents' totals,
rounds, wins and titles save between sessions. Complete qualifying, reopen the
game, and your grid and weather should still be there. Win titles and build a
strong reputation to found a racing team for two million expansion credits.

This is a text-based motorsport simulation inside MOD+; it does not replace the
original game's racing pack or add a 3D driving mode.

## Reality TV+

**MOD+ → Reality TV • seasons & production**

Start a named season as a contestant or producer from age 18. Choose Talent,
Survival, Social Strategy or Lifestyle. Play **eight episodes** with **nine rival
contestants**, evolving scores, alliances, audience and an elimination board.
Choose performance, relationships or extra production investment. Reach the
finale for a result and expansion payout, grow your audience and pitch a
licensed follow-up season. Seasons and unfinished episodes persist.

## Luxury spending and finances

**MOD+ → Luxury assets & annual accounts**

Buy a Recording Studio, Creator Campus, Luxury Yacht, Private Island, Art
Gallery, Racing Team HQ, Film Studio or Sports Academy. Purchases use expansion
credits. Assets have annual upkeep, changing valuation and declining condition;
refurbish or sell them. Condition affects income. Large holdings can have a
negative net result, so maintenance and liquidity matter.

Annual accounts show music royalties, creator income, net asset income and
expansion income tax. The first 10,000 of annual expansion income is tax-free;
15% applies to the next band up to 100,000 and 30% above that. A 3,500-credit
rookie stipend supports gradual progress. These taxes are part of this
expansion ledger and do not duplicate the main game's tax or touch its bank.

## More events when ageing

The native event pool has **60 additional four-choice scenes**, preserving all
1,708 previously authored expansion records. These cover school clubs,
independent artists, workplace decisions, online audiences, sports, reality
TV, luxury purchases, mentoring and later life. New decisions differ by scene.
Their appearance follows the main game's event selection rules.

In addition, each new year creates **three interactive MOD+ decisions** and
settles accounts once. Tap **Age-up decisions** when the MOD+ badge shows a
number. These choices affect expansion time, credits and reputation. The queue
is limited to 12 unresolved decisions, so deal with them regularly.

## Saves and rewinding

Every character has a separate expansion save. Visited-age checkpoints restore
when the main game's Time Machine returns to that age. A previously saved
future-age checkpoint is reused when you return there, preventing duplicate
annual payouts. There is no expansion checkpoint for ages before this system
first saw that character. Return to a supported visited age to resume; the
progress is kept. A living character and appropriate school/job/prison status
are required for eligible actions.
