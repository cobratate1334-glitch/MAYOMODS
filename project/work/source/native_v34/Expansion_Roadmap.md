# MayoMods community expansion ideas

These are saved concepts for gradual future development. They are not new
features in v34 and have no committed release dates. v34 implements navigation hardening, compact New Life controls, selected-relative context and all 102 existing game countries in national tournaments. The remaining systems below are pending.

| Concept | Requested gameplay |
| --- | --- |
| Mangaka and anime | Pitch original manga, choose Shonen/Seinen/Shoujo genres, hire assistants, manage weekly deadlines and unlock anime adaptations, studios, voice actors and royalties after major success. |
| TV direction | Direct series, miniseries and anime; negotiate network contracts, manage cast issues across seasons and maintain ratings to avoid cancellation. |
| Military special forces | Add country-specific elite units and fictional text missions with meaningful choices, gradually extending the existing military career. Suggested units include Brazil's BOPE/GRUMEC, US Navy SEALs/Delta Force and UK SAS. |
| Intelligence and science | Smarts-based agency applications and undercover/crisis missions; scientific careers involving classified projects, disease research and inventions. Suggested agencies include CIA, FBI and ABIN. |
| Countries and flags | Add missing sovereign countries and matching flags, with migration, sports and diplomacy support. Validate the game's country data and related systems before extending them. |
| Global politics | Country-specific offices, parties, campaigns and legislative choices; representative, senator and governor ladders alongside national leadership. Verify each country's government structure before implementation. |
| Youth soccer | Start local training at age six, academy scouting around age eight and progression through youth teams; professional contracts based on researched country rules. Community examples include Santos FC and a Brazil debut at sixteen. |
| Farming and agriculture | Buy land, tractors, harvesters and logistics trucks; grow crops such as soy and corn with climate/season effects, drought/frost losses, machinery costs and bankruptcy risk. |
| Police careers | Distinguish patrol/highway work from investigations, then add researched local elite units. Community examples include Polícia Militar, Polícia Civil, BOPE/CORE, SWAT and FBI HRT. |
| Superheroes and villains | Create powers, acquire lore-based abilities, train them, encounter rivals/allies and choose protection missions or fictional villain paths with reputation consequences. |
| Famous parents | Random or selected celebrity parents, inherited wealth, early fame, media events and career opportunities. |
| Royalty | Expand rulers' diplomacy, laws, parliament, scandals and military/state responsibilities through fictional text events. |
| Extended family tree | Track grandparents, great-grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins and nephews across marriages and generations in an interactive lineage. |
| Motorsport | Childhood karting, junior scouting and Formula 1/stock-car/MotoGP paths with race strategy, team radio, sponsors and rivalries. |
| International business | Regional franchises and global branches, takeovers, supply-chain events, lobbying and international rivals. |
| Campus politics | School/university campaigns, student government, student-body presidency, speeches, movements, principal negotiations and popularity. |

A sensible sequence is to stabilize navigation first, then prototype one
career using existing menu/save systems. Country-specific facts and rules
need research at implementation time. Each expansion should have persistent
state, clear entry/exit paths and recovery after canceled dialogs before a
public release. The listed institutions, brands and ages are community
suggestions, not verified implementation claims.

## Additional requests from this continuation

| Area | Next implementation scope |
| --- | --- |
| School life | Bullying, friendship support, reporting, conflict de-escalation and fictional school fights with consequences. Looks may influence some peer reactions, alongside personality, reputation and friendships; appearance alone must not determine treatment. |
| Family | Grandparents, aunts/uncles, cousins and persistent family links, with plausible parent-child age gaps and inheritance. Celebrity parents and God Mode selection follow once lineage is reliable. |
| Fictional shops | A direct in-game drug dealer with selectable inventory and game consequences; an expanded arms dealer with weapon categories, inventory, costs and ownership. These are fictional game systems. |
| Traits | Add original trait descriptions inspired by familiar life-sim archetypes, then connect them to actual choices, relationships and skills. Preserve the nine existing personality IDs when appending new choices. |
| Storm chasing | More preparation, forecasts, equipment, vehicle upgrades, route decisions, rescues, footage and contracts; additional visible weather/chase animation states. |
| Football | Youth school at six, scouting at eight, youth teams and country-specific professional entry rules, with permanent progression into existing professional leagues. |

Trait design candidates use original wording: ambitious (sets difficult goals), bookish (enjoys learning through reading), perfectionist (takes extra time to refine work), sociable (seeks conversation), reserved (prefers a small circle), family oriented (prioritizes relatives), outdoorsy (enjoys time outside), creative (looks for unusual solutions), practical (prefers workable plans), music lover (seeks musical activities), patient (tolerates slow progress), and competitive (enjoys measurable challenges). These are design candidates, not selectable v34 additions.

Official inspiration reference: https://help.ea.com/en/articles/the-sims/the-sims-4/skills/ . Descriptions above are newly written and do not reproduce EA trait descriptions.

Implement one durable system at a time, with a saved state, age eligibility, meaningful costs/rewards, Back/Close recovery and a way to resume after interruption. Research country-specific legal and institutional details before implementing them. No dates are committed.
