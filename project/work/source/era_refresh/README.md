Changes stay inside the original Unity menus:

- World Cup match cards resolve the current opponent's saved flag, including
  Croatia. Competition selection retains its original V1 competition logos.
- World News no longer clears pictures for years outside a short whitelist.
  Selected chronology images survive. Empty images get period art. Specific
  missing invention/event pictures receive archive media or supplied item art.
- Birth-year selection adds 1800, 1820, 1840, 1860 and 1880. There are 22 dated
  nineteenth-century headlines and five period summaries covering the century.
  The existing annual queue delivers them once per age.
- School menu icons and event pictures follow ten periods from the 1800s to
  2026. Supplied school art replaces unrelated or modern art in earlier eras.
  Existing school actions, eligibility and stable tile IDs remain intact.
- Shopping → Technology & style offers 29 dated items with pictures, real bank
  costs, saved ownership, age limits and visible use results. Future items are
  absent from current shop/collection pages. Retired items remain usable if
  owned. More options retains the classic collection and its existing saves.
- Four nineteenth-century day jobs pay actual money once per year through that
  menu. They do not replace the character's main occupation.
- Year-choice images use the Image override that Unity actually renders.
  Five new archive-art panels join the existing twelve panels. Repeat year
  callbacks keep the earlier repair.

Seventy-seven new Sprite resources are added: 61 selected supplied PNGs, eleven
attributed archive images and five crops for year buttons. Historical media
includes paintings, prints and photos, with source/license metadata; it is not
new AI history art. Supplied school/item images are game illustrations. Credits
are also available in the game under Technology & style → More options.

Thirty-seven automated cases cover native ARM64 boundaries with managed/Unity
API doubles, content/economic rules, original match flow, menu transitions and
all four restored war themes. Packaging checks resource types, dimensions,
pixels, old artwork and unrelated payloads. Signature and parser checks are
separate. These checks do not certify touchscreen, ART, Unity GPU rendering or
real saves on the owner's phone.

This does not implement the entire historical backlog. Profile reversion,
driving-test freezes, measured scrolling/1893 performance, broader prison and
military additions, social comments, Mod Creator and Yakuza remain open. The
1800s content is an extension of the existing simulation, not a complete rewrite
of every stock job, car, country law or random event. Game retail windows are
deliberate availability rules, not worldwide historical introduction claims.
