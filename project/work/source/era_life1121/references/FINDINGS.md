Game.Instance → Game.Life (+0x20) → tracker (+0x70), then sets the school-combat
byte at +0x639. The injected bridge instead followed Game.Hero (+0x28) → +0x70
and abandoned the newly created attack when that unrelated field was null.

## Actual local cases, money and outcomes

SimPast.GetOpenCrimesInPlaceOfResidence (0x2a88f34) and
IsOnTheRunFromPrisonInPlaceOfResidence (0x2a89368) drive the status. No crime or
conviction is fabricated for a meter. Surrender constructs ScenarioCrimeArrest
with the actual charge collection; it never clears charges. Native age guards
and original court/sentence handling apply. Crew attention is explicitly separate.

Food purchases recheck the actual double balance in Life.SimAssets, charge via
the original 0x2a6eb5c API, affect the hero and append a life-journal result.
Integer cents and a sub-nanocurrency comparison tolerance prevent 0.03 − 0.01
− 0.01 from incorrectly rejecting the final cent. A final tiny rounding residue
is charged as the remaining balance rather than creating fractional debt.
Unaffordable, child and fourth-serving requests do not spend money.

Bounty contracts are saved fictional cases for an actual adult period Bounty
Hunter. Witness/trail leads each count once. Backup requires both. A solo attempt
can fail with native health loss. Payment consumes the stage and age permit;
changing job/year/country, open local charges and stale steps block reward paths.
No custom bounty result pretends to be the game's original school fight engine.

precedes the service/roster helpers. Explicit service actions/snapshots still sync

and stock event pools are byte-identical to 1120.
