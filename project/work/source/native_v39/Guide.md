# MayoMods v39 — Worlds & Careers

Install **BitLife_MayoMods_v39.apk** over your existing MayoMods installation. The original signing identity is retained, with versionCode 1083. Keep the existing installation and saved lives.

Open **Activities → Community Expansions → Worlds & Careers**. There is also a direct Worlds & Careers entry in Activities. This release adds 367 text scenes, 425 character-scoped saved properties and 20 gameplay branches. The earlier manga/anime, TV, youth soccer, school elections, franchises, farming, shops, storm fieldwork and Stalker Path remain available.

Bank purchases and payments use the game's bank balance. Corporate transfer budgets, royal treasury and classified project funds are separate story budgets. Annual income has explicit age guards; one-time assets, support payments, IPOs, signings and endings have ownership or completion guards. Most branches share eight actions within that branch per age. Use **Resume saved chapter** if you left a random chapter unfinished. Both the roll and its threshold are saved before you leave the initiating choice.

The family tree groups ancestors, parents/cousins, a sibling's growing family and your spouse/descendants. Profiles open only after their discovery requirements are met. Unborn and memorial routes are separate, and each node retains parent/spouse links. These twenty people are named story relatives with aging offsets; they are not generated stock Relationships NPCs.

## Scope of this first release

These systems use the shipped saved scenario format alongside the base game. Career records do not create stock employers, professional team rosters, agency appointments or a new native planet/country. Court sentences and royal conflicts are story records. Famous-parent origins and alien origins are chosen after starting a life through Activities; they do not yet alter New Life birth generation, actual parent occupations or stock fame/inheritance. Corporate eras and the family tree follow this character; they do not yet persist as a native multi-generation company or genealogical graph after starting another character.

Youth soccer's existing age-six school, age-eight academy acceptance and saved youth/development agreement remain. The existing Brazil-sixteen/default-eighteen development threshold is retained; other countries' real employment rules and actual native pro-team debut still need work. The national tournament uses simplified story rules and its own four-age schedule rather than the official Olympic calendar or qualification regulations.

Publishing still uses preset concepts. Free-text manga pitching, native missing-sovereign-country/flag injection, true NPC family generation, country-by-country employment rules, stock prison integration, broader real-world unit coverage including a dedicated Delta Force profile, additional storm animation art and deeper native employment/celebrity integration remain later stages. Dangerous science, espionage, combat and escape content uses invented technology or abstract offscreen consequences, without real-world operational procedures.

The requested launcher, icons, fades, display controls and neutral Mod menu are retained. Rocket Games advertising, promotional links and marketing update prompts remain removed. New Life/back/surrender recovery and person-profile fixes are retained. All 23 native libraries and every DEX code item are byte-identical to v38; only the five launcher/diagnostic release labels change to v39.

```bash
python3 work/source/native_v39/content39.py BASE_V38_APK CONTENT_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY
python3 work/source/native_v39/release_labels39.py BASE_V38_APK
python3 work/source/native_v39/test_worlds39.py BASE_LIBIL2CPP_SO
python3 work/source/native_v39/build_v39.py BASE_V38_APK CONTENT_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY OUTPUT_DIRECTORY PRIVATE_KEY PRIVATE_CERT
```
