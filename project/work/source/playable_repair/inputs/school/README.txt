MAYOMODS SCHOOL LIFE - 48 ART ASSETS

24 transparent menu icons plus 24 complete event illustrations.
Themes: bullying, confrontation, getting help, friendship, exams, studying,
results, detention, cheerleading, sport, science, drama, music, art, chess,
and graduation.

The full ZIP is verified below 4,000,000 bytes.

IMPORT
All individual assets are PNG files. Icons preserve transparency and have
6 pixels of clear padding, with a maximum side of 256 pixels.
The event pictures are opaque 512 x 256 landscape illustrations.
Most pixels have been color-quantized to 256 colors for smaller files.
The larger WebP sheets are included for browsing when the budget permits.

PLACEMENT
Use MENU_AND_EVENT_MAP.csv to replace the generic icons and match art to
the named events shown in your screenshots. Import each individual file,
not the whole sheet, when displaying one event.
For a popup, put its event picture below the title and above the body text.
Fit the available panel width while preserving its 2:1 aspect ratio.
Use the transparent icons for menu rows at about 48-96 screen pixels.

These are illustrations for a contemporary school setting. The students in
the event pictures represent a scene; they are not custom player portraits.
The sport scenes use American football. Choose them for matching events.
The pictures are static artwork. Game code must load them into the menus
and popups; no APK or UI behavior is changed by this pack.

manifest.json lists every individual asset and its source-sheet coordinates.
generation_prompts.txt contains the exact prompts for matching future art.
