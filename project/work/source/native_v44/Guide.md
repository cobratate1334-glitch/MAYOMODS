# Mayo Mods v44 — Era Accessories

Open **Activities → Accessories Dealer**. The shop stocks accessories for your character’s calendar year. Bought pieces remain in that character’s wardrobe as the decades change.

## Starting a life

The New Life screen has an eighth compact control, **Year**. Choose a birth year from 1900 through 2020 by decade, or 2026. The other seven controls retain their existing functions. Starting a life commits the selected year. Each Age Up advances the calendar by the same number of years as the character’s age.

Existing saves begin in calendar year 2026 when first opened with this expansion; the character’s age stays the same.

| Years | Collection | Shop |
|---|---|---|
| 1900–1929 | Vintage | Mayo’s Millinery |
| 1930–1959 | Noir | Mayo’s Hat Parlour |
| 1960–1989 | Retro | Mayo’s Style Boutique |
| 1990 onward | Modern | Mayo’s Streetwear |

The calendar affects accessory stock, shop text and prices. Other careers, technology, laws, vehicles and events keep their existing game rules.

## Buying and wearing

- The collection includes all **23 supplied accessories** and four new original hats: Burgundy Cloche, Navy Homburg, Mustard Pillbox and Burgundy Snapback.
- Try on an accessory free. Finishing or dismissing the preview restores the saved outfit. Preview items are excluded from canonical portrait serialization.
- **Buy & wear** checks this year’s stock and your real game bank balance. An owned item is never charged a second time.
- Headwear and mouth accessories use independent slots. Mouth accessories become available at age 18.
- Older pieces return as vintage finds on a three-year rotation. Owned items remain wearable in every era.
- Cowboy hats appear in the US, Australia, Canada, Mexico, Brazil and Argentina.
- **My wardrobe → Saved outfits** stores three combinations for the current character. Empty slots do not erase the current outfit.
- Removing a mod hat restores any stock headwear that was temporarily replaced.

Ownership, the year and outfits use the game’s saved Genesis feature data. Portrait hooks recover equipped layers after native portrait rebuilding and aging.

## Installation and validation

The APK retains the existing Mayo Mods signing certificate and has version code **1088**. Install it as an update over v43 to retain the application’s existing data. The retained launcher/overlay can still display its earlier version label; the new **Year** control and **Accessories Dealer** identify this expansion.

Automated checks execute the new ARM64 routines with mocked managed/Unity boundaries, check the existing trait and gameplay bridges, round-trip the 27 native textures and verify the APK signature and entry alignment. The preview PNG is an alignment illustration made from the packed sprites. Android/ART execution and physical-phone Unity rendering have **not** been tested here.
