MayoMods v52 — New Life and stability
BitLife 3.25; Android versionCode 1096

This is a cumulative update to the released v51 build. The implementation and
automated checks are complete for the changes below. Full Android gameplay,
ART and Unity rendering have not been tested on a physical phone or emulator.
The release does not certify every button, league, event or device as bug-free.

Crashes, navigation and profiles

- Regions now reads the actual country field instead of interpreting the New
  Life person configuration as a country. City filtering validates collection
  bounds and actual string objects before inspecting them.
- Life journal now matches the button label displayed by the game.
- List open/close operations preserve their restoration coroutine and retain
  generation guards. Visible HUD accessibility groups are re-enabled even when
  the corresponding GameObjects stayed active.
- Appearance choices map to the matching style at the character's age and
  gender instead of using a nearby numeric ID. Active genetic styles take
  precedence over obsolete portrait-ID migration rules. These address causes
  of missing/black portraits; on-device portrait rendering still needs checking.
- Legacy modified scenario sheets use the native RandomSelection column and
  physical single-line CSV bodies. Existing Community Expansions and prior
  dealer/navigation repairs are retained.

New Life, eyes and the supplied assets

- New Life now has the original eight controls plus hair, hair colour, skin,
  ordinary eye colour, scars, eyepatches, facial hair and facial-hair colour.
  Genetics is distinct from ordinary Appearance. Asset/genetic pickers show a
  native portrait preview before Apply. Back discards the temporary choice.
- Ordinary eye-colour choices set both eyes to the same colour. Existing mixed
  ordinary palette IDs normalize to a matching pair. Complete, sectoral and
  central heterochromia use explicit Genetics choices with priority over old
  ordinary-eye overrides. Selecting an ordinary colour clears that eye trait.
- Native appearance-editor saves become the New Life base face. Independent
  scar, eyepatch and facial-hair layers remain separate.
- All seven available supplied sprite sheets remain included. The existing
  730 imported asset records are retained. 180 hair layers sharing 94 textures
  have been refitted to the native scalp and face anchors. Before/after decoded
  asset inspection sheets are included in the source archive; phone previews
  are the final check for clipping, colour, hairstyle fit and portrait caching.
- Generated non-newborn NPCs, including parents created during New Life, can
  receive genetic appearances and supplied hair. Adult-stage NPCs can also
  receive scars. These are stored on their own native face, not the hero's
  appearance preferences. Random generation is not guaranteed to select a
  rare trait for a particular person. Automatic age-zero assignment is excluded
  so an unchosen newborn hero trait does not override the New Life selection.

Family, height, calendar and variety

- Living parents with implausible ages relative to the hero are repaired to a
  plausible age gap. Valid and deceased-parent ages are preserved.
- Automatic adult-height estimates use both sex-adjusted parental heights,
  a smaller birth-country/cohort component and saved individual variation.
  Explicit chosen adult heights remain explicit. Native relationship profiles
  and social contacts use the same parent-height calculation.
- The official NCD-RisC 2016 dataset supplies means for 200 countries and birth
  cohorts from 1896 to 1996. The simulation interpolates selected historical
  cohorts and uses the nearest endpoint beyond them. Countries without a
  matched source use the fallback model. Individual spread and rare outliers
  are included; a national mean is not a maximum or a mandatory height.
- The model uses chosen individual SDs of 6.8 cm for male and 6.3 cm for female
  sampling, with a 2-in-1000 rare-tail component and broad 120–250 cm automatic
  bounds. These are game-model assumptions, not measured individual variance
  from the paper. The source's confidence intervals are not used as SDs.
- Social profiles now have six varied preference descriptions instead of the
  same taller-partner phrase. Existing height preferences retain their dating
  checks. New non-height descriptions do not implement a complete attraction
  simulation.
- Historical age rows display the corresponding year next to age. New Life
  retains year presets and adds a custom birth-year picker from 1 to 3000,
  adjusted by 1, 10 or 100 with preview, Apply and Back.

Activities with saved outcomes

- School has twelve situations: science, debate, newspaper, theatre, robotics,
  mentoring, student council, athletics, music, writing, gardening and exams.
  Each has distinct preparation, teamwork and shortcut choices. Subject skill,
  Smarts, inherited Genius, personality and approach affect the result. Outcomes
  save project experience and change the relevant actual game stat. Teamwork
  improves the chance at an energy cost; failure still grants experience.
- Fighter training improves striking, grappling, wrestling, conditioning and
  tactics, plus actual Athleticism. Recovery reduces fatigue. Three match plans
  and a repeatable circuit use skills, reach, Athleticism and fatigue. A win
  changes the actual bank balance; a loss affects Health. Circuit W-L is saved.
  The original Fight introduction and gym menus link to Training & fighter
  circuit. This circuit record is separate from the stock professional league.
- Church & community in Activities has services, choir, volunteering, reflection,
  fundraising and family visits. Activities save service history and affect
  Happiness, Karma, Willpower, Music or parental connections. The fundraiser
  checks funds before deducting 25. Supplied church imagery is used.
- Four underworld story activities save attention and crew trust; success and
  failure change actual balance, Karma or Happiness. Rising attention makes
  later attempts riskier. This is an added story system, not an integration
  with every stock crime or the native criminal-record system.
- Studio setup and practice use the existing studio's saved state. Practice
  improves Music and studio skill and advances an active project's progress
  and quality. Existing writing, recording, releases and royalties remain.
  Complete stock music/producer career playthroughs still need a phone check.
- Added community activities have an eight-action allowance per age. Age Up
  refreshes the allowance and reduces fatigue. Rejected school/fatigue/funds
  actions refund the allowance. Match-plan and status views stay available.
- Actual native ScenarioBase callbacks own each activity. Repeated or stale
  choices do not award duplicate results, and New Life releases old leases.
- Newly authored community scenarios display “Compiled by Atlanti”. The linked
  Atlanti documents and The Last Yakuza book were not imported into this build.

Social and sports preservation

- The native Facebook, Instagram, OnlyFans, TikTok, Twitch, Twitter, SoundCloud
  and YouTube row construction is restored across custom calendar years.
  Normal stock age/account requirements still apply. Existing Discord, Reddit,
  following, social connections, post/comment and profile routes are retained.
- Retained-feature tests exercise the prior social/post/comment, dealer, world,
  year, wardrobe and gameplay paths against the final native binary. The asset
  audit verifies all 102 nation chooser routes and existing competition content.
  This is not an end-to-end phone test of every World Cup, NFL, basketball,
  soccer or producer-career button. Those phone flows remain in the checklist.

What was verified

The verification JSON records exact hashes and check counts for the final build.
Native tests execute the patched ARM64 routines at zero and a relocated load
bias with modeled managed/Unity API boundaries. Content checks decode final
Unity resources, validate scenario/menu destinations and compare prior objects.
All existing Sprite objects and their geometry/reference data remain byte
identical. Only declared text assets and refitted hair pixels change in the
prior datapack, and six new text resources are registered. Every other packaged
native library remains byte identical to v51. APK signing, signer continuity,
entry comparisons and stored-library ZIP alignment are verified.

Compatibility and installation

This build requires arm64-v8a and Android API 25+ (Android 7.1+); target SDK is
35. It is not a 32-bit or x86 build and has not been tested across all Android
devices. Stored .so entries have 16 KB ZIP alignment. The retained
libAsyncGPUReadbackPlugin.so ELF layout still limits 16 KB-page devices;
ZIP alignment alone does not repair that library. The compatibility report
lists the actual manifest and ELF requirements.

The APK retains the v51 signing identity. Back up game saves before installing
an update. Devices running a differently signed build require its matching
update path; uninstalling may remove local saves.

Phone checks still required

1. Start New Life, preview/apply/cancel each asset and genetic category, save a
   stock appearance edit, then choose country, gender, Regions and custom year.
2. Verify matched ordinary eyes and complete/sectoral/central heterochromia on
   the hero and edited NPCs. Age through infant, child, teen, adult and elderly;
   inspect the portrait, profile and hair fit after each transition and reload.
3. Open/close Activities, Community Expansions, occupation, achievements,
   social profiles and native events. Check the top menu and bottom tabs after
   Back, dismissal, resume and Age Up. Open Life journal.
4. Check parent ages/heights in both native and social profiles, explicit
   height persistence and NPC preference variety across several new lives.
5. Open each social platform, create a post, view/reply to comments and follow
   an existing contact. Confirm stock account and age rules behave normally.
6. Choose a nation and play/resume World Cup and club competitions. Exercise
   soccer, basketball and NFL career menus and their Back paths.
7. Play distinct school choices, church activities, fighter training/recovery
   and bouts, underworld stories and a full music project. Reload and compare
   the saved stats, balance and activity allowance. Rapid taps must not pay twice.
8. Keep the v52 crash diagnostics if a phone-only failure remains. Full fatal
   exception/tombstone context is more useful than the preceding warning lines.
